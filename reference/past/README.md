# Past IndusTrack graphics (reference only, do not republish as-is)

Shared by the user on 2026-10-01. Square (1080x1080) and story (1080x1920) versions of the same designs.

## Worth keeping (style and ideas)
- "We could serve residential. We chose commercial. 15 years in." Strongest positioning line: one message, confident, commercial-first.
- "10 years. No annual contract. They stayed because it works." (CurbCo): proof with a bridge from the number to why it matters.
- "$0/month" as one huge figure: instant stop-the-scroll for the free office.
- "15 Years. The feedback never changes. Too expensive. Too complex. Nobody answers.": a strong pain setup.
- Visual elements that worked: orange left edge bar, subtle diagonal line texture, "TRUSTED FOR 15 YEARS" pill, big quote marks for proof posts, one oversized headline, story versions alongside squares.

## Retired or off-rule (never reuse)
- "free forever" (several designs): banned. Use "No cost for your office" / "Start free. Upgrade when your field team needs mobile access."
- "Free data migration" / "We migrate your data for you" / "Free data migration on upgrade": we do NOT offer free migration.
- ServiceTitan $2,500 vs $625 comparison, "Save $10,500+": no competitor names, prices or savings in social.
- Fake dashboard with real customer names (CurbCo, Sabre Plumbing) and invented numbers: no fake UI, use real screenshots.
- Em dashes in copy; "Inventory" listed as a free feature (not in the free-tier list); "4 things" headline with only 3 bullets.
- Demo links on cold social graphics (cold posts use Start Free).
